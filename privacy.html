Privacy Policy

Stack'd Minimalist · effective 2026-10-20 · applies from version 1.5.0

Stack'd is a to-do, notes and meetings app. It has no accounts and no analytics. Everything you write stays on your device unless you press one of the two buttons described below.

What stays on your device

Boards, tasks, notes, meetings, transcripts, minutes, action items and your settings are stored only on the device you use, in the app's own storage. Recordings are stored on the device too. Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no sync and no backup to any server run by us.

What leaves your device, and when

Two actions in the Meetings area send data to our server, and only when you press them:

ButtonWhat is sentWhere it goes
Transcribethe recording you choseour server, which passes it to a speech-to-text provider and returns the transcript
Summarisethe transcript you chose, with the speaker names you gaveour server, which passes it to an AI model provider and returns the minutes and action items

Our server keeps none of it: no audio, no transcript, no minutes are stored after the result is returned. It keeps only a counter of how much of your monthly allowance you have used, tied to an anonymous subscriber identifier, and error logs that contain no content. The providers process the data to produce the result and are contractually bound not to use it to train their models. The app tells you this before the first time you press either button, and you can read it again under Settings › Meetings & AI.

Nothing is sent automatically: recording a meeting, playing it back, pasting or attaching a transcript and editing anything all happen on the device.

Recording

The app uses the microphone only while you are recording, after you tap Record and after you grant the system permission. It shows a recording indicator, and the system shows its own. Make sure everyone present knows and agrees to be recorded; the laws on this vary by country.

Subscriptions

Plus, Pro and Max are bought through the App Store or Google Play, which handle payment and keep your payment details; we never see them. To know which plan you have, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service, which stores an anonymous subscriber identifier generated by the app and the purchase information the store shares with it. That identifier is the one our server uses for your monthly allowance. Restoring a purchase on a new device uses the store account, not any data of ours.

Reminders

Task reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No server is involved.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we knowingly collect no data from them.
