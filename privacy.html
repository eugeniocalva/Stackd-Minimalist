Privacy Policy

Stack'd Minimalist · effective 2026-11-16 · applies from version 1.6.0

Stack'd is a to-do, notes and meetings app. It works without an account: then everything stays on your device and nothing is stored on our server. With an account, your boards, tasks, notes and meetings are also kept on our server so they are the same on your phone and your computer. There are no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Recordings are never stored on our server: they stay on the device that made them. The PIN locks the app; it does not encrypt your data on the device or on the server. Your data is encrypted while it travels between your device and our server.

We use this data only to provide the account and sync. The legal basis is the contract with you (the account you asked for). Account emails are the sign-in code, "new sign-in to your account", "your PIN was changed", "your PIN was reset" and "your account was deleted". We send no newsletter and no marketing.

Shared boards

When you share a board, the people you invite see and change its cards and lanes, and see your email address. When you are on someone else's board, its owner and the other people on it see your email address and what you add. An invitation keeps the invited email address until it is accepted, declined, withdrawn, or 30 days have passed. The board and its cards are stored on our server until its owner deletes it; when the owner deletes their account, the boards they shared are deleted too.

Transcribe and Summarise

Two buttons in the Meetings area send data for processing, only when you press them:

ButtonWhat is sentWhere it goes
Transcribethe recording you choseour server, which passes it to a speech-to-text provider and returns the transcript
Summarisethe transcript you chose, with the speaker names you gaveour server, which passes it to an AI model provider and returns the minutes and action items

Neither step keeps the recording, the transcript or the minutes on our server after the result comes back; the providers process them to produce the result and are contractually bound not to use them to train their models. If you are signed in, the transcript and minutes that come back are then part of the meeting and are stored by sync like the rest of it. The server keeps a count of how much of your monthly allowance you used, and error logs that contain no content, no email address and no network address. The app shows this before the first time you press either button; you can read it again in Settings › Meetings & AI.

Recording

The app uses the microphone only while you are recording, after you tap Record and after you grant the system permission. It shows a recording indicator, and the system shows its own. Make sure everyone present knows and agrees to be recorded; the laws on this vary by country.

Reminders

Task reminders are notifications scheduled on each of your devices. No server sends them.

Your rights

You can see and correct your data in the app, at any time. In Settings › Account you can export everything we hold about you as one file (Export my data), sign out any device, and delete your account and everything synced to it (Delete account); the deletion is immediate. If you cannot open the app, see how to delete your account. You can also write to us to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, or to receive it in a portable form, and you have the right to complain to a data protection authority, in particular [OWNER: the supervisory authority of your establishment] or the one where you live.

Age
