Delete your Stack'd account

Stack'd Minimalist · applies from version 1.6.0

In the app

  1. Open Stack'd on any device where you are signed in (iPhone, Android or the web app).
  2. Go to Settings › Account › Delete account.
  3. Choose whether to keep a copy of your data on that device.
  4. Enter your PIN (if it is locked or forgotten, reset it first with an email code), then type DELETE to confirm.

The deletion is immediate. You receive one last email to confirm it.

What is deleted

  • Your account: your email address, your PIN data, the record of what you accepted, and the list of your devices.
  • Everything synced to it: boards, tasks, notes and meetings with their transcripts, minutes and actions, priority levels, labels and settings.
  • Boards you shared, for everyone on them, and your place on boards other people shared with you.
  • Invitations you sent or received.

Not deleted: data you chose to keep on a device, and recordings, which are never on our server and stay on the device that made them. Every other device you were signed in on signs itself out the next time it connects.

Subscriptions

Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the App Store (Settings › your name › Subscriptions) or in Google Play (Play Store › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions).

If you cannot open the app

Write to hi@stackdplatform.com from the email address of the account, with "Delete my account" in the subject. We confirm that the address is yours, delete the account and everything listed above, and reply within 30 days.
